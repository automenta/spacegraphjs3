Interaction Guide

  • Hover over edges to highlight them
  • Click on edges to select them
  • Ctrl+Click to multi-select edges
  • Hover over nodes to highlight them
  • Click on nodes to select them
  • Ctrl+Click to multi-select nodes
  • Drag nodes to reposition them
  • Drag background to pan the view
